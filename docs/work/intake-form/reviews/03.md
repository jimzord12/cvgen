## Findings

### F1 Material: follow-up cap contradicts the form shape this change teaches
Anchor: docs/guides/client-workflow.md:25 and 208-210; .claude/skills/new-client/SKILL.md:51; guide section 2 (lines 79-83).
Scenario: step 7 allows "aim for 5, never more than 10", "optionally as an `Intake Form`". Section 2 now says a form is designed with pages by topic and quick question types. The evidence form, `client-form-01` (titled "λίγα στοιχεία ακόμα", so a follow-up), has 27 questions plus consent across 8 pages.
Expected: one rule a later session can follow. Actual: sending this form breaks a "never" rule that is still active in the guide, the step table and the skill. A grid of 8 rows makes "a question" ambiguous.
Impact: sessions either ship forms that break the rule or cut the forms back to the text-box shape the owner rejected.
Smallest fix: the lead's plan. Put it to the owner and record his answer. Then align the table row, section 7 and skill step 7 (for example a time budget of about 10 minutes, or a cap on free-text questions only). Until then, do not send the form as it is.

### F2 Minor: theme steps state unverified UI as fact
Anchor: docs/guides/client-workflow.md:91-95.
I could not confirm that Forms' font menu has a "More fonts" entry, or that it offers Bona Nova or Source Sans 3. My understanding (uncertain) is that Forms offers a fixed, curated font list. Uploading a header can also reset the theme colour; the order given (Header before Color) handles that.
Fix: confirm these on the owner's first run, and add a fallback ("if a font is not offered, keep the default").

### F3 Minor: jumps can only skip ahead; some jump mistakes pass the check
Anchor: scripts/intake-form.gs:25, 31, 115-117, 146, 157.
Scenario 1: "Ναι -> A, Όχι -> B" with page A before page B. People who answer Ναι also pass through B. That needs an end-of-page jump (`PageBreakItem.setGoToPage`), which the engine cannot express.
Scenario 2: two pages share an `id`. The check passes and the later page silently wins.
Scenario 3: a jump targets a page at or before its own choice. The check passes, so a respondent can loop.
The current forms are unaffected.
Fix: add one line to the kinds comment ("a jump skips ahead; later pages follow in order"). Make `checkForm_` reject duplicate ids and targets that do not come after the choice.

### N1 Note: "the same questions as a Relay message" is now loose
Anchor: docs/glossary.md:52, guide line 77. A form now reshapes the questions and also serves step 7. Suggested wording: "the Relay questions (or the follow-up) as a designed Google Form".

### N2 Note: unsupported keys are silently ignored
Examples: `other` on a `dropdown`, `validate` on anything but `short`, `id` off a page, a typo such as `goto`. A `goTo` choice that is not `required` lets a blank answer fall through to the next page. The client form sets `required: true`, so it is safe. Optionally reject unknown keys for each kind.

### N3 Note: limits of the evidence
The stand-in accepts any method name, so the API names rest on reading, not on a run. The owner's first real run is still the proof, as in round 1 (N2). The masked `client-form-01.txt` still shows a Latin first name ("Paulos"). It sits under ignored `builds/`, so there is nothing to fix.
