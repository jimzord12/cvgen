## Findings

### F1 Material: the design-reviewer now blocks every non-CV design it already reviews
Anchor: `docs/review.md:19-21`; `.claude/agents/design-reviewer.md:3, 61-69, 96-99`
- **Scenario:** review.md still sends "brand or design assets" to the design-reviewer, now "judged first on the `Batch Test`, the `Three-Second Test` and Flagship parity". Its recent uses include the logo (`docs/work/brand-logo`), the `Text Draft` (`premium-text-draft`), the intake-form header and Flagship layout work (`short-career-layout`).
- **Actual:** under the new profile, criteria 1-3 are Blocking "however well made". A logo or a `Text Draft` cannot shout a `Domain`, specialty and role, and it is never scanned in an HR batch. A Flagship layout change fails criterion 4 ("could not be mistaken for Flagship") by construction. The new description also narrows the scope to "concept runs and real client CV designs", which review.md contradicts.
- **Impact:** the next brand, text-draft or Flagship round blocks for the wrong reason, or the reviewer improvises.
- **Fix:** scope criteria 1-4 to CV pages. For other assets, name the bar: premium, on-brand and fit for purpose; for a Flagship change, parity with no regression. Mirror that in the review.md sentence.

### F2 Material: the client-design review path is referenced but not defined, and its rubric blocks by construction
Anchor: `design-reviewer.md:15-16, 37-39, 70-72, 98`; `.claude/skills/new-cv/SKILL.md:56-61`; `docs/guides/client-workflow.md:271-274`
- **Scenario:** the tour-leader client (client-2026-09-01) goes through this path next.
- **The loop does not exist:** the design-reviewer says the loop for client designs is "in the `new-cv` skill". new-cv gives it one sentence. It has no step to copy the revision PDFs from `private/` to `builds/` (the reviewer may not read `private/`), no brief contents (`Domain`, specialty and role, the chosen concept as source), no round cap and no `unresolved` exit. It also says nothing about where the reports go, and a report on a real CV stored under `docs/work/` would be public.
- **The rubric blocks by construction:** new-cv builds the one-off `Template` from the style the owner picked. Criterion 4 and "a page that could be mistaken for its source" are Blocking, so a faithful client design fails. Criterion 8 ("become a template") does not apply either.
- **Fix:**
  - Add three or four lines to new-cv covering the copy to `builds/design-review-input-<ts>/`, the brief (steer plus chosen concept folder), the cap (5 attended, 10 unattended) and report storage (client id, never the name, or inside the `Envelope`).
  - In the rubric, for client designs, turn criterion 4 into "distinct from the market, faithful to the chosen concept", and mark criterion 8 n/a.

### F3 Minor: the editor's contact sheet is outside its write fence
Anchor: `magazine-editor.md:20-23` vs `:145-148`
The fence allows writes only to style folders, the README and fonts, but the self-check asks for a contact sheet under `builds/`. The editor either skips the one self-check aimed at the business tests or breaks its fence. The idea-run git-status guard cannot see `builds/` because it is ignored. The same shape was rated Minor in `docs/work/idea-agents/reviews/04-code-reviewer.md` R2-1.
Fix: add "and scratch renders under `builds/`" to the fence.

### F4 Minor: `design-concepts/README.md` still describes the old folder shape
Anchor: `design-concepts/README.md:3, 8-15`
It still describes "one-page mock-up", "`concept.typ`, `concept.pdf`, `page-1.png`" and a compile line for `concept.typ`. AGENTS.md now says new runs use `safe/stylish/creative` files. This header is prose outside the idea gates (review.md:51-56), so this change owns it.
Fix: describe both shapes and give the per-tier compile line.

### F5 Minor: the batch sheet set-up is ambiguous for nine pages
Anchor: `design-reviewer.md:46-52, 61-62`
"Put each page under review among them on one contact sheet" plus "the page is the one the eye lands on" can only hold for one page if all nine share a sheet. Safe would then always lose to Creative.
Fix: one sheet per page, or say that each page is compared only with the plain CVs.

### N1 Note: "style" and "Flagship parity" are recurring, unnamed terms
"Style" is the unit of a run in five files, sits close to `Theme`, and is also called "concept". "Flagship parity" appears in review.md, idea-run and the design-reviewer. Glossary rule 3 suggests adding terms.
