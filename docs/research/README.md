# Research Library

Read this before researching for a client (`docs/guides/client-workflow.md`,
steps 4 and 6). Shared, dated research notes that more than one client can
use: CV norms of a country, what a `Domain` or a `Rank` expects, how CV
writers work. Public, so it never holds anything about a client.

## A note

One topic per file, named `<scope>-<topic>.md`: `germany-cv-norms.md`,
`marine-chief-officer-expectations.md`, `cv-intake-practice.md`. It opens
with these lines, then the findings:

```markdown
# <Topic>

Checked: 2026-09-25. Recheck after: 2027-03-25.
Question: <what the note answers>
Review: <research-reviewer rounds and verdict, with the report paths>
```

Findings are numbered claims, each with a source `[S#]`, the source's date,
and a label when it is a vendor claim or an opinion. Sources are listed at
the end.

## Rules

- **Only reviewed research enters.** A note or a change to one lands after
  a `research-reviewer` PASS; its reports go under
  `docs/work/<task or client-free id>/reviews/`.
- **Six months, then recheck.** Past its "Recheck after" date, a note is used
  only after its load-bearing claims are checked again; then both dates
  move and the note records what changed.
- **No client data.** No names, employers, dates of service or anything that
  points to a person, even when a client's case started the research. A
  finding about one employer stays in that client's `Envelope` until a
  second client needs it.
- **One note per topic.** Update the existing note rather than starting a
  parallel one.
