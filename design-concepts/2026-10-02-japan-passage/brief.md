# Japan Passage

Three words: **crafted Japanese journeys**. One two-page `Spacious` design at the `Stylish` `Design Tier`, proposed on 2026-10-02. `Domain`: Travel & Tourism (`travel` in the `Meta File`); specialty Japan; `Rank`: Αρχηγός-Συνοδός Εκδρομών. This is a design proposal, not an adopted production `Template`.

The owner explicitly narrowed this run to one design and recalibrated toward professional, clean, well-organized and premium, with controlled creativity. That brief takes precedence over the idea agent's usual eighteen designs and its earlier creative intensity. There are no Condensed, Safe or Creative variants in this run.

## What to notice first

A confident dark identity field, a crafted serif name and one integrated original Japan-and-escort drawing: Fuji, a torii, a small travel arc, and an escort holding a flag with the group. The drawing carries the specialty and the actual role; the page below remains a straightforward professional document. Both pages repeat this identity, with a shorter header on page 2. Cherry branches and petals at 8.5–11% opacity occupy the blank lower outer margin, away from factual text. One clean red Japan seal accompanies the languages; it is a decorative destination mark, never an official credential.

The Marine Flagship in Golden Blue is the professional foundation: clear high-contrast identity, a calm white factual field, brass numbered section headings, aligned dates and honest metrics, consistent spacing, and domain artwork that belongs to the composition. Its preferred balance comes from the relationship between these devices, not merely navy and gold. Here the name uses a Greek serif rather than Flagship's condensed display, cinnabar supplies the Japanese/travel accent, and original escort artwork replaces the marine tools and portrait. This changes the identity without copying Flagship's hero arrangement.

This primary collection article shows distinctive serif lettering and illustration working together in compact travel identities. Principle taken: a strong serif identity with selective place imagery. No hotel label, logo, layout or artwork is reproduced. Its collage/luggage surface is deliberately excluded.
5. **JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles, Eki stamps**: https://www.japanhousela.com/articles/station-to-station-japans-iconic-eki-stamps-train-stamp-book-passport-goshuin-goshuincho/. The article describes often circular, place-specific station stamps with landscapes or landmarks. Principle taken: a single compact destination mark. Our simple 日本 seal is fictional decoration, not a real station stamp, passport stamp or religious seal.
6. **CVgen public role research**: [`greece-japan-tour-leaders.md`](../../docs/research/greece-japan-tour-leaders.md). Principle: keep dated Japan experience, practical know-how, operations, group responsibilities and earned credentials findable. No new employment or licensing claim is introduced.

These references span marine professional documents, travel ephemera, scenic role storytelling and archival typography; they contribute distinct principles. The owner specifically requested a blend of the internal references, so resemblance in professional hierarchy and travel cues is intentional. All illustrative SVG paths are original vector work. Japanese strings come verbatim from the fictional record except 日本, already used in the reviewed Stamp Rally designs; no new translation is asserted. ATH → TYO is a symbolic destination cue, not a claimed itinerary or flight booking.

## Data and page plan

`sample.json` is copied byte for byte from Stamp Rally's fictional record, SHA-256 `ae4f753744c2a0521ba6a22802cc23d5e7ef086f71c80653bdc651f06b3b4746`. No real client or private data was read. Page 1 carries identity, contacts, full profile, Japan trips and both jobs with all responsibility points. Page 2 carries all six groups, all destination/operations skills, three education records including issuer and hours, languages, first aid including issuer/hours/expiry, all availability points and the offered pre-departure briefing.

Totals are computed from arrays: five Japan stays/trips, 103 days in Japan; six groups, 162 passenger places, 39 days of escort work. The record does not establish that all 162 passenger places were unique people. Only the April 2026 Japan group was co-led; five Europe groups were led. The Japan stays include travel and study, never presented as five led tours. JLPT N4 is earned (12/2024); N3 remains a target (12/2026). No licence as a local guide is implied.

The 11pt body remains fixed. Factual tables use 9.5–10pt, Japanese place-name supplements 8pt, and decorative/secondary labels 7.2–9.5pt. Pagination and spacings are explicitly authored; nothing automatically shrinks. No portrait is needed: the escort drawing is the identity's visual centre.
