## Findings

### F1 Minor: the Compare "✕ Close Esc" button still closes the Loupe as well
Anchor: `scripts/design_review/index.html:177` (`data-close`), `:542` (`closeAll`)
- Scenario: in the Loupe, press T, then click Compare's Close button instead of pressing Esc.
- Expected: the button labelled Esc does the same as Esc and returns to the Loupe.
- Actual: `closeAll` hides both overlays, so the round-1 F2 "loses his place" case remains through the mouse.
- Fix: give Compare's close button the same handler as the Esc branch (hide `#compare`, restore overflow only when the Loupe is hidden).

### F2 Minor: Rescan hides the state-file error toast immediately
Anchor: `index.html:223-225`
- Actual: on a non-quiet load, the `stateError` toast is replaced in the same tick by "N designs indexed", a 3.5 s non-error toast.
- The first load and focus reloads are quiet, so they do show the error, and every save attempt toasts "Not saved: … cannot be read". There is no data risk.
- Fix: skip the success toast when `j.stateError` is set.

### N1 Note: the export labels some present files "No longer found (moved or deleted)"
Anchor: `server.py:181-184, 250-253`
- With `--no-private`, every client review lands under that heading.
- So does a PDF skipped as unreadable during a compile.
- A neutral label ("Not in this index") would be accurate.

### N2 Note: the glossary row puts `Board` and `Loupe` in backticks, but they are not terms
Anchor: `docs/glossary.md:78`
- Rule 2 uses backticks to mark official terms.
- Either add the two words as rows or drop their backticks.

### N3 Note: coverage and evidence of the fixes
- The UI fixes (Compare keys, Loupe rescan, flat strip) are not covered by any automated test. This was accepted in round 1 as N1.
- The screenshots in `builds/` (23:41) predate both commits.
- The skip of an unreadable PDF and the plain-compile LOCK are untested.
- The shared `LOCK` means the first Batch compile blocks state saves for its duration (they wait and are not lost).
