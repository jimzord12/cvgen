## Findings

### F1 Minor: the step table states half the rule
Anchor: docs/guides/client-workflow.md:25
- Scenario: a session reads only the table: "or a form of at most 15 minutes".
- Expected: the owner's rule is about 15 minutes and at most 5 typed questions.
- Actual: the typed-question limit is missing, and "about" has become a hard "at most".
- Impact: the table allows a 15-minute form with 10 paragraph questions.
- Fix: "... or a form of about 15 minutes with at most 5 typed answers".

### F2 Minor: skill step 7 can be read as both caps applying to forms
Anchor: .claude/skills/new-client/SKILL.md:50-52
- Scenario: "aim for 5, never more than 10; as an `Intake Form`, at most about 15 minutes..."
- Expected: guide section 7 is explicit that the form cap "is effort, not a count".
- Actual: the skill never says which count applies to chat. A reader can take "never more than 10" as universal, which reproduces round 3's F1 for the 25-question form.
- Fix: "as a chat message: aim for 5, never more than 10; as an `Intake Form`, no count: at most about 15 minutes and at most 5 typed answers".

### F3 Minor: section 2 still says "the same questions, beside the chat message"
Anchor: docs/guides/client-workflow.md:76-77; docs/proposals/client-workflow.md, 2026-09-28 decision ("never instead of it").
- Scenario: section 7 sends a follow-up form "or" a pasted message, and points to section 2 for how to make it. Section 2 describes a form that repeats the chat message's questions and goes beside it.
- Impact: a 25-question follow-up form cannot equal a chat message of 10 or fewer. It is unclear whether a chat version must accompany it. Round 3's N1 anchored this line too, but only the glossary was reworded.
- Fix: one clause in section 2: "at the follow-up, the form follows section 7's effort cap". Also state whether the chat message then only carries the link. I am unsure whether "never instead" was meant to cover follow-ups; the owner can settle that in a sentence.

### N1 Note: nothing says how to judge "about 15 minutes"
The example form states "περίπου 10 λεπτά". The client form's description gives no time. Suggestion: Claude states its estimate in the description and in the report. Also say whether an optional "Other" box counts as typing (the client form has 2).

### N2 Note: keys are checked globally, not per kind
My probes pass silently: `other` on a dropdown (round 3's own N2 example), `validate` on a `long`, `id` on a non-page. Nothing breaks, because the keys are ignored. Optional: per-kind key lists.

### N3 Note: `in` sees prototype names
- A page id `constructor` throws a false "Two pages share the id". This fails safe, before anything is created.
- A goTo target `toString` with no such page passes the check (the comparison with a function is NaN, so false). It would then fail inside Google after `FormApp.create`.
- Improbable with the ids in use. Fix: `Object.create(null)` or `hasOwnProperty`.

### N4 Note: the dry run's "jump backwards" case trips on its first choice
That first choice jumps to its own page, so the Q-to-P backward jump is never reached. My probe tested both cases separately. Both throw.

Correctness confirmed:
- A choice's index can never equal a page's index. A jump to the choice's own page gives `pageAt < index` and is rejected.
- A jump to the next page, or from before the first page to the first page, passes.
- The options check runs before the `goTo` checks use `options`.
- `required: 'yes'` is rejected.
- KEYS covers every key the kinds comment documents.
