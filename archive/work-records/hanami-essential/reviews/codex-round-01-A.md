First impression: A strong pink nameplate above a substantial, clearly organised CV; the blossom corner and portrait give it a recognisable identity.

Scan Test: Name first, current title and aligned dates next, employer directly underneath. The second job follows the same structure; numbered Education is easy to locate at the bottom.

- **1 — Batch Test: PASS.** The centre tile attracts attention through its broad header, decisive typography and full-page organisation. Its distinction survives thumbnail reduction.
- **2 — Scan Test: PASS.** Job titles, employers and dates occupy predictable positions. The support example remains subordinate to the employment heading.
- **3 — Flagship Parity: PASS.** Comparable control of alignment, typography, fine rules and accent colours. The quieter artwork is appropriate to this variation.
- **4 — Distinct: PASS.** Sakura, plum typography, cream paper and bronze details retain Hanami’s identity. The organising structure is conventional, but the complete treatment is sufficiently individual.
- **5 — Reads as a CV: PASS.** Identity, role, current employment and credentials are accessible. Filled versus open destination markers preserve their distinction without colour.
- **6 — Craft: PASS.** Clean column boundaries, readable text and consistent date alignment; no visible clipping or collisions.
- **D1 Note (Craft):** Nit: the large gaps before the internship and Education make the left column’s rhythm uneven beside the continuous sidebar. **Fix:** redistribute a little of that space around the section transitions.
