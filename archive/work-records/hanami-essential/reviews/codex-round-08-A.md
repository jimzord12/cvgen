## Shared findings

**Round 8 looks more finished at the top, but slightly more padded through the main column.** The highlight row earns its place; the expanded vertical gaps contribute less.

- **D1 Note (Craft):** Across all four pages, the gap before Reservations Agent and the gap before Education are conspicuously generous beside the tightly packed experience bullets. They are broadly even, but feel allocated to fill height rather than determined by the content. **Fix:** Cap those gaps at a smaller, consistent section interval and allow more whitespace after Education. Matching the sidebar’s depth is unnecessary.

- **D2 Note (Scan Test / hierarchy):** The highlights are useful as a rapid summary, particularly the annual trip volume, country count and qualification. “On the road / The single contact, seven days a week” is the weakest item because it repeats both the introduction’s central promise and an experience bullet. **Fix:** Shorten that supporting line to make its availability information more immediate. No additional fact or replacement content is needed.

- **D3 Note (Craft):** Destination dates and durations remain crowded, especially “September 2024 14 days.” **Fix:** Add a modest, consistent horizontal gap between these fields.

The remaining space below Education looks intentional, not unfinished. Filling it further would weaken the page. The four body layouts are effectively identical, so they tie on padding: there is no defensible best or worst body-spacing variant. **Greyscale has the strongest overall finish; plain-long-name has the least visual activity in its header, but remains complete.**

No visible alignment, clipping, collision or hierarchy defect appears in these renders. The earlier images are unavailable, so a direct visual regression comparison cannot be made.

Criterion 7 is excluded by the brief; criterion 8 does not apply.
