- **2 — Scan Test: PASS.** Both jobs use predictable title/employer/date positions. Education is easy to locate and read.
- **3 — Flagship Parity: PASS.** Typography, alignment and restrained rules carry the premium impression. The shorter content leaves considerable lower-page space, but the fixed footer and bounded sidebar keep the composition intentional.
- **4 — Distinct and faithful: PASS.** The condensed name, numbered sections, diamond bullets, inset notices and typography retain Hanami’s identity. The route arc is a modest travel cue. This reads as **general-purpose and professional**, without becoming an anonymous office template. Earlier concepts and Anti-examples were not supplied; comparison with them is unassessed.
- **5 — Reads as a CV: PASS.** Identity, role, current employment and qualifications are immediately available. The visual hierarchy already works in neutral tones.
- **6 — Craft: PASS.** Clean column alignment, consistent indents and controlled wrapping. Black headings remain decisive against the warm greys. Bold bullet labels help distinguish responsibilities without making entire paragraphs feel bold.
