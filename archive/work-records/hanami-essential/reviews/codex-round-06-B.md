## client CV: page 1: PASS

First impression: An illustrated travel identity with a commanding nameplate; the portrait and monumental torii immediately distinguish it from the surrounding CVs.

Three-Second Test: **PASS.** Travel: aircraft, departure/arrival arrangement and travellers with luggage. Japan: torii, Fuji and cherry blossom. Tour Leader: the flag-bearing figure visibly leading the travelling group. The rank is conveyed by that relationship, without needing the role tab.

- **Batch Test — PASS:** Row 2, left is the unmistakable destination for the eye. The architectural header, portrait and oversized name interrupt the surrounding text-led pages.
- **Flagship Parity — PASS:** Comparable confidence and bespoke illustration. Flagship has stronger large-area contrast; Hanami compensates with its layered header and emphatic typography. The warm greys look deliberate, and the nameplate remains authoritative without pink.
- **Distinct — PASS:** Faithful to the supplied pink concept and clearly differentiated from the market templates shown. The journey, group leader and Japanese architecture form one coherent idea. Earlier concepts and Anti-examples were not supplied; those comparisons cannot be assessed.
- **Reads as a CV — PASS:** Name, role, current position and JLPT credential are readily located. The A4-shaped composition retains a clear employment hierarchy. Essential information does not depend on colour.
- **Craft — PASS:** Strong alignment, controlled text columns and readable contrast. No visible clipping or collisions. The pale background drawings remain subordinate, while the stamp and black role tab provide crisp accents.
- **D1 Note (Craft):** The portrait is visibly **still coloured**: blue background, warm skin and a blue-grey shirt. This contradicts the brief’s description of a greyscale portrait, although it does not make the page muddy or diminish its overall quality. **Fix:** Apply the monochrome treatment to the portrait, preserving separation between face, shirt and background.

## client CV: page 2: PASS

First impression: A substantial illustrated continuation, with two bold travel stamps anchoring the upper body and large section numbers organising the remaining content.

Three-Second Test: **PASS — continuation.** The repeated name typography, route, flag-led travellers, torii, Fuji, wave pattern and stamps unmistakably connect this page to page 1.

- **Batch Test — PASS:** Row 3, middle stands apart through its illustrated header, paired circular stamps and full-page sectional rhythm. It remains identifiable without the portrait.
- **Flagship Parity — PASS:** The deliberate stamp pairing and structured lower sections sustain the premium character. This is a designed second page, not a generic overflow sheet.
- **Distinct — PASS:** The same Japan travel identity continues coherently. No supplied pink page 2, earlier concepts or Anti-examples are available for direct comparison.
- **Reads as a CV — PASS:** Trip evidence, practical competence, education and languages are clearly separated and quickly found.
- **Craft — PASS:** Dense but legible. Black stamps and headings establish sufficient contrast against the light artwork. Education sits close to the footer, but remains cleanly separated with no visible collision.
