## generic-portrait: PASS

First impression: A substantial nameplate, restrained portrait medallion and clearly organised editorial page.

Scan Test: Name first; role directly beneath; current title and dates share a prominent row, employer immediately below. The second job repeats that placement. Numbered Education is easy to locate within the first scan.

- **Batch Test — Pass:** The warm header, strong typography and substantial right panel make this recognisable among the eight comparators. Its distinction comes from composition and order as well as the portrait.
- **Flagship Parity — Pass:** Comparable confidence in alignment, type hierarchy and detailing under the craft-only comparison. The quieter treatment suits this brief.
- **Distinct — Pass:** Condensed display lettering, numbered sections, diamond bullets, warm paper tones and ruled details retain a credible connection to Hanami. The result is broadly applicable and professional without becoming a bland template.
- **Reads as a CV — Pass:** Employment remains the dominant content. Credentials and supporting information have predictable locations; the design remains legible without colour.
- **Craft — Pass:** The cut-out sits convincingly on its plate. Hair and shoulders show no conspicuous extraction halo at the supplied resolution. The shoulders terminate naturally at the circular crop, and the head has enough breathing room. Scale balances the name rather than competing with it.

The colour portrait adds warmth and a human focal point. Its pale blue shirt introduces a small cool accent, but does not disrupt the restrained header.

## generic-portrait-grey: PASS

First impression: The most tonally unified version; portrait, header and typography feel conceived together.

Scan Test: The same clear name → role → current job/employer/dates → previous job → education sequence as the colour version.

- **Batch Test — Pass:** The page retains its visual presence without colour in the portrait.
- **Flagship Parity — Pass:** Hierarchy, spacing and portrait framing maintain the required craft standard.
- **Distinct — Pass:** The same Hanami-derived structure and detailing remain evident.
- **Reads as a CV — Pass:** Greyscale removes no information or navigational cue.
- **Craft — Pass:** Facial features and hair remain distinct from the plate. The white shirt separates adequately from the surrounding grey; the crop and ring remain balanced.

**My preference for Monochrome is greyscale.** It integrates the portrait more completely into the page and lets the name lead. Colour is also publishable: choose it if personal warmth matters more than tonal consistency. This is a preference, not a pass/fail distinction.
