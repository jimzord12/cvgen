First impression: A confident pink masthead and sakura illustration distinguish a compact, orderly CV; the pale right rail makes its structure immediately apparent.

Scan Test: Name first, then the two bold job titles, their employers immediately underneath, and dates consistently aligned right; education follows in a clearly numbered section. All required fields are readily located within approximately five to eight seconds.

- **1 — Batch Test: PASS.** The sixth tile stands out through its broad pink header, strong name treatment and coordinated sidebar. Its hierarchy remains visible at batch size. Under the specified bar, it earns attention through composition and identity.
- **2 — Scan Test: PASS.** The fixed positions work. Dates belong unmistakably to their jobs, employer labels are distinct from descriptions, and education requires no searching. The short profile leads directly into the content.
- **3 — Flagship Parity: PASS.** Comparing craft, the page has similarly controlled alignment, typographic hierarchy, restrained rules and integrated illustration. Its reduced visual complexity suits this variation and its thin record.
- **4 — Distinct and faithful: PASS.** Sakura artwork, dusty pink, plum typography, copper section numbering and the red role label preserve Hanami’s identity. Together they lift the familiar two-column structure beyond a generic template. Earlier concepts and Anti-examples were not attached, so those comparisons cannot be assessed.
- **5 — Reads as a CV: PASS.** Name, role, current employment, languages and degree are easy to locate. The supplied A4 composition reads clearly. Weight, placement and rules carry the hierarchy independently of colour; black-and-white reproduction appears viable, although no grayscale render was supplied.
- **6 — Craft: PASS.** No visible overflow, collision or stranded separator. The large lower vacancy reads as the consequence of a short record: both columns finish at a similar height, the footer anchors the page, and content has not been inflated to fill space. The full-height pale rail gives that vacancy a consistent structure. This is calm and deliberate rather than broken.
