## Findings
### F1 Material: handover cannot complete for a client whose `Domain` does not exist yet
Anchor: client-workflow.md:181-186; new-client SKILL.md:46-48; build-a-cv.md:225-230; validate.py:35-36 (pinned by tests/run.py:48)
Scenario: the tour guide signs off. The agent follows step 9, then new-cv, then build-a-cv section 8. Section 8 says to import `lib.typ` and keep the record valid against "the schema".
Expected: a one-off `Template` that goes through `cv.py render`, `Approval` and `Export`.
Actual: a cv.typ that imports `lib.typ` and no other domain is checked against the Flagship (marine) input schema. I called the same function render.py:34 calls, with a tour-guide record, and got REFUSED: `'companies' is a required property`, `'experience' was unexpected`. The check is skipped only when cv.typ imports neither `lib.typ` nor a domain path, for example by importing a one-off template file kept in the `Envelope`. That file is not snapshotted into the Revision (render.py:46). None of this is written down, and section 8 and new-cv cover marine only.
Impact: the guide's own example client stalls at the first render. The owner's decision stands; the wiring behind it does not exist yet.
Fix: in step 9 and in new-cv, describe the path that works today, or say that a CV for a new `Domain` waits for the framework-split card. Log the `Framework Gap` now.

### F2 Material: the Deep Dive review sends research-reviewer into `private/`, and its reports can publish the client's name
Anchor: new-client SKILL.md:30-37; guide:144-154; research-reviewer.md:14-16, 22-23, 62; docs/research/README.md:28-30
Scenario: a Deep Dive is written to `private/maria-papadaki-tour-guide/research/deep-dive-x.md`, then the skill calls "a fresh research-reviewer per round".
Expected: the loop runs within the reviewer's own rules, and its reports are stored without client data.
Actual: research-reviewer.md says "Never read `private/`" (the agent has web search) and names idea-run as its only loop. The skill does not say to pass the text inline, what snapshot id to use, or where reports for Envelope-only Deep Dives go. Reports for findings that go into the `Research Library` are stored under `docs/work/<id>/reviews/`, and the reviewer copies the brief's "Snapshot:" line into them. If the brief gives the Envelope path, the client's name and `Rank` land in the public repo. The same risk applies to handoff cards and commits, where the folder name is the natural way to refer to a client.
Impact: the loop stalls or the reviewer breaks its own rule, and the reports are a likely leak route.
Fix: in the skill and the guide, pass the Deep Dive text inline after checking it for client details, with a client-free snapshot id (a hash). Keep reports for Envelope-only Deep Dives in the Envelope's `research/`. Add a rule that the Envelope folder name is client data: outside `private/`, use a neutral alias. Add one line to research-reviewer.md naming this loop and the rule against searching for a person (an edit, not a new agent profile).

### F3 Material: consent comes last and gates only research, against the approved proposal and the skill
Anchor: guide:90-97; SKILL.md:16-20; proposals/client-workflow.md:34
Scenario: the client sends a CV and certificate photos the first evening, then "I agree" two days later, or never.
Expected: the approved proposal says "A consent line comes first". The consent text asks permission to "use AI tools … to write your CV".
Actual: consent is question 13 of 13. The guide blocks only research, so step 3 (AI reads the documents into facts.md) can run before consent. The skill blocks everything until consent arrives. The acceptance criterion says the skill must be "consistent with the guide".
Impact: AI can read a client's personal documents before they consent.
Fix: make consent item 1. In the guide: "Nothing after step 2, facts included, starts before 'I agree' is in messages.md."
