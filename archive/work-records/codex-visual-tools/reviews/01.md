### F2 Material: isolation depends on a prompt and three remembered flags
Anchor: proposal lines 51-56

Fix: in the plan, add `--ignore-user-config` (auth still works, per the help), plus `-c project_doc_max_bytes=0` or run from a directory outside the repository. For image generation, scope `-C` to the target folder. Keep the prompt instruction as a second layer.
