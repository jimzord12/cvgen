## codex-06-lineup.md (unedited)

# Codex visual review round 2

Snapshot: Five styles, Stylish / Condensed / page 1; colour above, monochrome below. Thumbnail lineup review only.
Batch sheets: n/a — the supplied lineup is not a batch sheet.

## Lineup of five styles: PASS

First impression: Poster Sun catches the eye first through its large illustrated header; Timetable holds attention through the strongest name treatment and the most decisive organisation of the information.

**Distinct — PASS.** The five pages have visibly different organising ideas: a navigational chart, an airmail envelope, a transport timetable, a travel poster and an engraved document. None could readily be mistaken for another.

Rhumb Lines and Poster Sun share the most conspicuous individual device: a circular portrait with radiating lines. They do not share an organising idea. Rhumb Lines spreads delicate cartography around paper panels; Poster Sun builds a substantial landscape header above a compact column layout.

Airmail and Guilloche remain related through document imagery, but their silhouettes separate them. Airmail encloses the entire page in postal stripes and uses a stamp-like portrait. Guilloche concentrates its identity in an engraved band, arched portrait and rosette, with fine decorative rules continuing into the body. It now reads more as an engraved travel document than an identity card.

The compass, postal border and retro landscape are familiar travel motifs. Their integration into different page structures gives them sufficient distinction here; they are not interchangeable decorative badges. Guilloche has the weakest connection between its dominant header idea and its body, although that connection is visible.

Earlier concepts and Anti-examples are not attached; resemblance to those cannot be assessed.

**Colour ranking — stopping power combined with professional, generic travel positioning**

1. **Timetable.** Best combined result. The emphatic name, yellow section bands and aligned information make the candidate’s record the main visual event. Its transport reference supports a professional travel identity without suggesting a particular destination.
2. **Poster Sun.** Strongest immediate attraction and clearest illustrated travel presence. The landscape feels geographically broad. It places second because the illustration dominates the thumbnail more than the employment information does.
3. **Airmail.** Strong recognition, a clear name block and a coherent postal composition. The border still attracts substantial attention, but the interior has enough structure to compete.
4. **Rhumb Lines.** A distinctive, carefully drawn compass and elegant title give it character. The fine rules, pale paper and relatively delicate typography produce less authority at this scale; the imagery also suggests navigation more specifically than general travel consulting.
5. **Guilloche.** The engraved header is recognisable and composed, but the pastel tones and fine body structure give it the least stopping power among these siblings.

**Monochrome ranking — quality as independent black-and-white designs**

1. **Timetable.** Strong black section bars, clear white intervals and light table bands establish the most convincing tonal hierarchy.
2. **Poster Sun.** The dark sea becomes a purposeful graphic anchor. The white sky, mountain tones and radial portrait remain distinct.
3. **Airmail.** The single-ink stripes feel intentional and retain the envelope identity. Black title and section accents give the interior a matching visual vocabulary.
4. **Rhumb Lines.** The compass becomes crisp and expressive, with the chart remaining subordinate. Its body is noticeably lighter than its upper artwork.
5. **Guilloche.** The arch and rosette survive clearly, without obvious muddy fills at this scale. However, the thin engraving and pale header provide the weakest tonal hierarchy.

- D1 Note (Distinct): Guilloche’s strongest identity remains concentrated above the body. Fix: slightly strengthen the existing engraved section rules so the organising idea carries farther down the page.
- D2 Note (Batch Test impression): Airmail’s striped perimeter remains a strong competitor to its interior hierarchy, especially in monochrome. Fix: reduce the stripes’ dark coverage slightly.
