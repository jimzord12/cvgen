## Guilloche, colour: FINDINGS
First impression: a rainbow-tinted engraved header with an arched portrait, the name and a globe rosette. It stands out on its sheet and no longer says "passport".
Scan Test: name in under 1 s (now in reading order); jobs with mono dates flush right in about 2 s; education in about 3 s.
- D19 Blocking (6 Craft, 3 Flagship Parity): the bottom 15% of the page is empty. The body ends at about 238 mm and the footer microprint sits at 281 mm, leaving about 43 mm of blank paper across the full width. The engraved centre rule runs on for about 30 mm past both columns, so it bounds nothing. At arm's length the page looks unfinished, heavy at the top and hollow at the foot, and it is the only one of the five that does. Fix, smallest first: end the centre rule with the content, then spend the space by opening the section spacing, or by making the header band about 15 mm deeper so the portrait and the rosette get more room. Moving the footer up only hides the problem.
- D20 Note (4): the labelled field grid (SPECIALTY / DATE OF BIRTH / NATIONALITY / PASSPORT) is the last document cue left. It is fine at this strength; do not add more.

## Verdict: FINDINGS
Eight designs pass: Rhumb Lines, Airmail, Timetable and Poster Sun, in colour and in mono. Every round-1 Blocking item from both judges is fixed, and what is left on them is Nits (D15, D17, D18) and Notes for the owner (D2, D5, D10, D16).
