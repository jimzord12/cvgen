## Why Golden Blue works

Its navy/teal/brass palette is purposeful: dark ink and a substantial opening band establish authority, teal distinguishes secondary information, and brass is reserved for crafted artwork, section numbers and fine rules. Colour is a hierarchy, not independent decoration on every object.

The portrait, name plate and engineering frame form one focal composition. The 83 mm opening band is expressive; the body returns immediately to a white field, 16 mm side margins and a repeatable date/content grid. Body type is Source Sans 3 at 10.5 pt; Barlow gives names and numbers a clear display voice. Aligned dates, companies, vessel rows and durations reduce the reader's work.

Pale technical edge drawings extend the professional identity without competing with text. Page two repeats the compact identity, rules, numbered sections and factual rhythm, rather than repeating the large portrait hero. The finish comes from consistency, hierarchy and custom artwork together.

## Retain, simplify, remove

- **Retain:** Flagship's authoritative opening, readable typography, white factual field, disciplined spacing, numbered hierarchy and edge artwork. Stamp Rally's Japanese travel-stamp principle and flag-led group identity. Woodblock Road's selective Japan silhouette and nature; Concourse's organized facts and role clarity.
- **Simplify:** one coherent Japan/escort emblem and a restrained seal accent; straight, aligned trip and group records; a small purposeful colour vocabulary. Nature becomes pale branch/petal artwork outside text, with no large scenic painting.
- **Remove:** faux barcodes, guilloche behind text, torn receipts, rotated documents, luggage tags, repeated credential stamps, multicolour ink coding, LED boards, giant arrows, vertical names and dominant patterned backgrounds.

The new concept should read first as a serious CV, then reveal a crafted Japanese travel identity. Both pages must retain enough visual travel/Japan/escort information to stand independently in the Batch Test and Three-Second Test. Owner taste remains the final decision.
