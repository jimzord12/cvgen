# brand

CVgen's own brand assets, not client or template material.

## logos/

- `CVgen_Logo_high_res.jpg`: the working logo, chosen by the owner on
  2026-09-25 "for now". A big black serif "CV" with a thin rose-copper wire
  that weaves through the C and the V and ends as a small "gen".
  It is an image-model render (2816x1536 JPEG) shown as a 3D object on
  textured paper, so it is a mock-up, not a production file.

Still needed before wide use (card `brand-logo` on the Trello board):

1. A flat vector redraw (SVG): logo only, transparent background, no metal
   texture or shadows.
2. A `CV`-only mark for small sizes (favicon, chat avatar), where "gen" is
   too thin to read.
3. Colour values for the ink and the copper wire, recorded here.
